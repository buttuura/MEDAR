About Us - Who We Are

Building a safe, supportive community for Ugandan migrants and returnees

Our Story

Formed in 2022 by Ugandan migrant workers across the Middle East, Europe, and the Gulf, the Forum was born from lived experience. A group of Ugandans, having navigated the challenges of migration firsthand, came together to create a safe space for migrant workers, diaspora members, and returnees. Our goal is simple: to ensure every Ugandan journeying abroad or returning home is met with dignity, support, and guidance for safe landing and successful resettlement. Today we unite voices across the diaspora to share resources, advocacy, and community.

Our Vision

To prepare a safe and successful landing/stay for migrant workers, travellers and returnees.

Why We Exist

  • 68% of Ugandan migrant workers report lack of pre-departure support
  • 1 in 3 returnees face reintegration challenges with no network
  • Over 50% encounter contract disputes or exploitation abroad

We Support

Providing practical guidance, resources, and connections to protect and assist migrants at every step.

We Empower

Building skills, knowledge, and confidence for self-reliance and lasting reintegration back home.
