Who We Are

We Care. We Support. We Empower.

The Forum for Uganda's Migrant Employees, Diaspora and Returnees is a community-driven initiative formed to protect, guide and empower Ugandans at every stage of their migration journey.

From pre-departure to reintegration, we provide practical support, advocacy and a reliable network to ensure dignity and safety for every member.

Our 8 Activities

Trusted support services for migrants, diaspora and returnees

Airport Picking & Dropping

Safe and reliable airport transfers for arriving and departing members.

Hotel Booking & Travel Docs

Assistance with passports, visas, air tickets and accommodation.

Legal Support

Contract reviews, rights protection and access to legal guidance.

Job Connections

Connecting members to safe, vetted local and international opportunities.

Rental Management

Property management for diaspora members while abroad.

Buy Plots & Construction

Employment & Construction Guidance

Career guidance, CV support and project supervision.

Counseling & Psycho-Social Support
