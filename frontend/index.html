Who We Are

We Care. We Support. We Empower.

Migrant Employees, Diaspora and Returnees is a community-driven initiative formed to protect, guide and empower Ugandans at every stage of their migration journey.

From pre-departure to reintegration, we provide practical support, advocacy and a reliable network to ensure dignity and safety for every member.
