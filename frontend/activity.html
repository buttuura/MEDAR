Our Activities

Practical support for a safe and successful journey, stay, and return home.

Here for every step

Support that makes a difference

Explore the services available to migrants, diaspora members, and returnees.

Airport Picking and Dropping

Safe transportation to and from the airport for arriving and departing migrants and returnees. We ensure you reach home safely.

  • Entebbe Airport pickup 24/7
  • Safe transit to Kitara Center
  • Family reunion support

Hotel Booking & Travel Documents

Hotel booking and tour sites identification, travel document processing e.g passport, visa, air tickets.

  • Passport & Visa processing
  • Hotel booking worldwide
  • Tour & travel guidance

Legal Support & Contract Guidance

Legal support for rights violation and contract guidance for workers abroad and locally. We fight for your rights.

  • Contract review before signing
  • Rights violation reporting
  • Connection to lawyers

Buy Plots & Construct

Buy plots and construct on behalf of migrant workers pay in installments. Your money is safe while abroad.

  • Verified land titles
  • Pay in installments
  • Construction supervision with photos/videos

Job Connections

Job connections both local and international across multiple industries. We connect you to safe, verified jobs.

  • Vetted recruitment agencies
  • Local & international jobs
  • No exploitation

Rental & Tenant Management

Rental and tenant management for properties owned by diaspora members. We manage while you are abroad.

  • Collect rent monthly
  • Property maintenance
  • Monthly report on WhatsApp

Employment & Construction Management

Job/employment guidance and construction management support and supervision for your projects.

  • CV & interview guidance
  • Site supervision
  • Trusted contractors

Counseling & Psycho-social Support

Counseling/psycho-social support and rehabilitation of returnees. You are not alone.

  • Trauma counseling
  • Returnee rehabilitation
  • Family mediation

Savings & Member Credit

Boosting savings and offering credit to our members.

  • Support members' savings goals
  • Credit access for members

Need assistance?

We are here to help you every step of the way.
